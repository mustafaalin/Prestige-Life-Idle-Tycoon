Data requests — Prestige Life: Idle Tycoon

Provider: Mustafa ALIN / Math Games Studio. Updated: 22 October 2026.

There is no registered player account in the current Android game. You can still request access to or deletion of personal data processed in connection with the Game.

Email mathgamesstudio@gmail.com with the subject “Prestige Life — Data request”. Explain what you want deleted or accessed. If available, include your Player ID from the Game's Settings. For a purchase issue, you may include the Google Play order number. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or identity documents unless a proportionate verification step is specifically explained.

We assess the request, verify it where necessary, and locate relevant support and provider records. The Player ID identifies RevenueCat records; it does not automatically identify every Firebase or advertising record. We may need additional information and will explain what can be located, deleted, or retained for legal reasons. We respond within applicable statutory periods; KVKK applications are concluded within 30 days. Records needed for legal obligations, fraud prevention or legal claims may be retained for the applicable period and purpose.

To erase local progress, use Android Settings → Apps → Prestige Life → Storage → Clear storage/data (wording varies). This is destructive. Check Android/Google backup settings separately because backed-up data can be restored. Clearing local data does not itself delete provider records or refund purchases. Eligible permanent purchases may be restored; consumed cash packs are not a cloud backup of your game balance.

Google also provides advertising controls in Android settings. For Google-held data and store records, consult https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy. We cannot promise to erase independent records that Google must retain.
