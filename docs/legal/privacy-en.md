# Privacy Policy — Prestige Life: Idle Tycoon

Last updated: 22 October 2026

## 1. Who is responsible

Prestige Life: Idle Tycoon (the Game; Android package com.prestigelife.idletycoon) is published by Mustafa ALIN under the Math Games Studio brand. Mustafa ALIN is responsible for the processing described here. Math Games Studio is a brand name, not a separate incorporated company.

Privacy and support contact: mathgamesstudio@gmail.com.

This notice covers the Android game. A visit to our website may also involve the website host's connection logs and website analytics; these are separate from the Game's SDKs.

## 2. Local game data and accounts

You do not create a player account or sign in to play. Progress, balances, story choices, character names, settings and local reminder preferences are stored on your device. We do not operate a cloud-save server for this version. Our configured gameplay analytics do not include the names you enter for characters. Some progress milestones and actions are nevertheless sent as analytics events, as described below.

Android backup or device-transfer features may copy and restore app data, depending on your device and Google settings. Uninstalling or clearing local data does not necessarily delete operating-system backups or information already processed by our service providers. A pseudonymous SDK identifier is not the same as a registered player account, and does not make the associated information anonymous in the legal sense.

## 3. Data processed by the Game and its providers

Google Analytics for Firebase: app-instance and available advertising identifiers; device, operating system, app version and language information; approximate region inferred from network information; sessions, screen views and gameplay events. Events include progression, businesses, careers, purchases, advertising interactions and notification interactions. We use these to understand usage and improve balance and usability. We do not request GPS location permission.

Firebase Crashlytics: crash and non-fatal error reports, exception messages and stack traces, app/device information, installation identifiers and diagnostic context. Reports help us find and fix failures. Our app forwards uncaught JavaScript errors and rejected promises as well as native crash information. We do not intentionally attach character names or full game saves to these reports.

Google AdMob and its advertising partners: IP address, approximate location, advertising/device identifiers, ad and app interactions and diagnostic information. These are used to deliver and measure advertising, prevent fraud and, where permitted by your choices and applicable rules, personalise ads. The current game offers optional rewarded ads. Choosing not to watch an ad is not a guarantee that no SDK data is processed.

Google Play and RevenueCat: purchased products, transaction and purchase-token information, purchase status, timestamps, prices/currency, and a pseudonymous RevenueCat App User ID. These support purchase validation, delivery, restoration, fraud prevention and purchase analytics. The Game sends its Firebase app-instance ID to RevenueCat to support the configured purchase-analytics integration. Payment details are handled by Google Play and its payment providers; we do not receive your full payment-card number or security code through the Game.

Support: if you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any identifiers, receipts or screenshots you choose to provide. We use these to answer your request, investigate purchases and handle privacy requests. Please do not send passwords, full card details or unnecessary identity documents.

Notifications: reminders are scheduled locally if you grant permission. This version does not use a remote push-notification service. Permission decisions and notification opens may be recorded as analytics events.

The Game does not request access to contacts, camera, microphone, photos or precise location. This does not prevent approximate location being inferred from an IP address.

## 6. Choices and permissions

In regions where Google's consent system requires it (for example the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the Game asks for your choices after the opening story, and you can change them later in Settings → Privacy options. These choices cover advertising and also whether Google Analytics for Firebase may store data on your device: if you do not consent, Analytics may only send limited measurement signals without persistent identifiers. Outside these regions analytics is on by default. Crash reporting (Crashlytics) does not depend on these choices, and the current version has no separate switch for it.

You can manage your advertising identifier in Android settings and turn reminders off in the Game or device notification settings. You may play without watching rewarded ads. The Comfort Pack removes the need to view rewarded ads for its benefits; it does not promise to disable analytics, crash reports or purchase processing.

## 7. Retention and deletion

Local saves remain until cleared or removed, subject to Android backup/restore behaviour. Analytics retention depends on the property's configured event/user retention and any setting that renews retention on activity; aggregated reports may have different retention. We do not promise that all analytics data disappears after a fixed 14-month period.

Google states that Crashlytics retains crash traces and associated identifiers for 90 days before starting removal from live and backup systems. Purchase records are retained as needed to provide entitlements, investigate transactions and fulfil applicable tax, accounting, fraud-prevention and legal obligations. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to resolve requests and applicable disputes or obligations. Advertising providers apply their own applicable retention rules and consent controls.

To request access or deletion, email mathgamesstudio@gmail.com with the subject “Prestige Life — Data request”. If available, provide the Player ID displayed in the Game's settings and the scope of your request. We may ask for proportionate information to locate records and verify the request. A RevenueCat Player ID does not by itself identify every Firebase or advertising record. We will explain any identification limitations or required retention, rather than promise deletion of records we cannot locate or control.

There is no registered game account to delete. Clearing game progress does not automatically delete provider records, cancel a purchase or produce a refund. We respond within applicable legal time limits; under Türkiye's Law No. 6698, applications are concluded as soon as possible and within 30 days. Some rights may have legal exceptions.

## 8. Children and teenagers

The Game is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. Local consent and parental-authorisation requirements may use a higher age; a 13+ audience declaration does not replace those requirements. If you believe a child has provided personal data inappropriately, contact us so we can investigate and take the required action.

## 9. Security and your rights

Connections to the providers described above use encryption in transit. No system can guarantee absolute security. Depending on your location, you may have rights to information, access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection, portability, withdrawal of consent and complaint to a supervisory authority.

Under Article 11 of Türkiye's Law No. 6698, rights include learning whether data is processed, obtaining information about processing and recipients, requesting correction or deletion when statutory conditions apply and notification to recipients, objecting to an adverse result based solely on automated analysis, and claiming compensation for unlawful processing. Send requests to the contact above; using a right will not remove statutory purchase or consumer protections.
